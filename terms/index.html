3. AI-made content

PretendThat uses artificial intelligence services from OpenAI and Anthropic to talk with your family and to make each game’s story, play guide and printable pages. Nutmeg is a pretend character made by a computer, not a real person. We use kid-safety filters and checks, but AI can still make mistakes: something may be inaccurate, odd, or not quite right for your family. Please look over printables and play ideas before kids use them, and skip anything that doesn’t suit you. If something seems wrong, tell us (see section 7).

4. Privacy

We don’t have accounts, ads or ad tracking. Your child’s voice is turned into words right on the device and is never sent anywhere or saved. Only those words, with names replaced, go through our relay to our AI partners so Nutmeg can answer and make the game; the relay doesn’t log them. Our Privacy Policy explains what is sent to our AI partners and what is saved on your device.
