1. Who it’s for

PretendThat is a pretend-play helper for kids about 4 to 8 years old, set up and managed by a parent or other grown-up. The grown-up who sets up the app agrees to these terms for the family. Kids should use PretendThat with a grown-up nearby. Play happens in your home, with your things, so please choose safe props and a safe space, and keep an eye on the fun.

2. Plans and billing

  • Free Preview: a small number of free new shows (planned: 2), counted by the app. No payment details are needed. It is not an Apple free trial.
  • Show Pass and Season Pass are auto-renewing subscriptions sold through Apple’s App Store. The price, length and number of new shows each month are shown before you buy. Replays of saved shows are included.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • Auto-renewal: your subscription renews automatically at the same price and length unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple ID is charged within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Cancelling: manage or cancel any time in your Apple ID settings (Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions), or in the app under Grown-ups, then Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t issue refunds ourselves.
  • Monthly limits: each plan includes a set number of new shows a month. The planned limits are 2 shows for the Free Preview, up to 10 a month with the Show Pass and up to 30 a month with the Season Pass; these are planned and may change before launch. The limits in effect are shown in the app before you buy. Unused shows don’t carry over.
  • Restoring: to use your plan on another iPad with the same Apple ID, use Grown-ups, then Restore purchases.

3. AI-made content

PretendThat uses artificial intelligence services from OpenAI and Anthropic to talk with your family and to make each show’s story, play guide and printable pages. Nutmeg is a pretend character made by a computer, not a real person. We use kid-safety filters and checks, but AI can still make mistakes: something may be inaccurate, odd, or not quite right for your family. Please look over printables and play ideas before kids use them, and skip anything that doesn’t suit you. If something seems wrong, tell us (see section 7).

4. Privacy

We don’t have accounts, ads or tracking. Your child’s voice is turned into words right on the iPad and is never sent anywhere or saved. Only those words, with names replaced, go through our relay to our AI partners so Nutmeg can answer and make the show; the relay doesn’t log them. Our Privacy Policy explains what is sent to our AI partners and what is saved on your iPad.
