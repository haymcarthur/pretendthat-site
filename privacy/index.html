Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

PretendThat is an app for iPad and iPhone for kids ages 4 to 8. We built it so families can use it without giving us anything about their kids. This page explains, in plain language, what the app and this website do with information.

The short version

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • We never ask for or collect kids’ names. Nutmeg calls kids by their roles, like “the Captain” or “the Head Baker”.
  • Kids’ voices are turned into words right on the device and never leave it. Only the words, with names replaced, go to our AI partners so Nutmeg can answer.
  • Saved games keep only the game itself, on your device.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Accounts

PretendThat has no accounts, sign-in or profiles. We don’t have a database of users.

Kids’ voices and what they say

Your child’s voice stays on the device. Nutmeg only listens while the microphone button is held down. What your child says is turned into words right on the device by Apple’s on-device speech recognition, and the recording is thrown away the moment the button is let go. The sound of your child’s voice is never sent to us, to our AI partners or to anyone else, and it is never saved.

Only the words go to our AI partners. To answer, the app sends those words, with names (like “my name is …”) replaced by “[name]”, through our relay to OpenAI, which creates Nutmeg’s reply and her voice, and the game details built from them to Anthropic for the story. The words are checked for kid safety on the device first. PretendThat doesn’t keep a transcript, and the chat on screen disappears when the game ends.

Microphone and speech permissions. iPadOS and iOS ask for both. Apple’s standard speech permission message includes a sentence about speech data being sent to Apple; PretendThat requires on-device recognition, so your child’s voice is not sent. If the device can’t recognize speech on the device, the microphone stays off and you can type instead.

Nutmeg is instructed never to ask for or repeat personal information, and the app watches for things like phone numbers, emails and addresses and steers away from them.

How the AI parts work

To create a game, the app sends game details (the kind of game, the theme, roles, props and the kit to draw) to AI services from Anthropic (Claude, for the story and play guide) and OpenAI (for the pictures, safety checks and Nutmeg’s voice). These requests, and the words for Nutmeg’s replies, go through our own relay, a small server that holds our service keys and forwards each request. The relay is stateless: it doesn’t log or store what passes through it. No audio of kids’ voices ever goes through it. It runs on Cloudflare, which handles connection details such as your IP address to deliver requests and to limit how often they can be made; we don’t keep them.

Saved games

If you tap the star to save a game, the app stores the game content on your device: its title, roles, props, kit pictures, play guide, a cover picture and Nutmeg’s own recorded lines (her voice only). It never stores kids’ names, kids’ voices, what anyone said, a chat transcript or how many kids played.

Saved games stay on your device. We plan to add syncing through iCloud later; if we do, saved games will sync through your family’s own private iCloud account, not to us. You can delete saved games any time in the app’s Grown-ups corner, and deleting the app removes them.

No ads, tracking or analytics

The app has no ads, no advertising or tracking tools, and no analytics. We don’t sell or share information for advertising.

Purchases

Any purchases or subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your payment details. For billing help or refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Kids’ privacy

PretendThat is made for kids, and we designed it with children’s privacy laws such as COPPA in mind: we don’t ask kids for personal information and we don’t knowingly collect it. A grown-up gate (a press-and-hold plus a number question) protects the Grown-ups corner. If you believe your child has sent us personal information, for example through our support form, email us and we’ll delete it.

This website and the support form

This website has no ads, analytics or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services receive standard connection details (such as your IP address) when you visit.

If you use the support form, your name (optional), email address, topic and message are delivered to our support email through the FormSubmit service. We use them only to answer you, and we’ll delete them on request.

Changes

If we change how PretendThat handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@pretendthat.app or use the contact form.
