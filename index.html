How it works

From idea to showtime in four steps

Kids do the talking. A grown-up just helps with the printer.

1

Pick a game by talking

No reading or typing needed. Kids tell Nutmeg what they want to play, like “a bakery on the moon”, and she offers ideas, asks how many kids are playing (up to 6), and gives everyone a role.

2

Find props at home

Nutmeg sends kids on a prop hunt, one thing at a time: a bowl becomes a mixing cauldron, a blanket becomes a stage curtain. Don’t have something? Tap ✗ and she swaps it or you pretend.

3

Print a custom kit

A kit drawn just for this game: role badges, a sign, play money, tickets, a menu and cut-outs. Choose full color, or plain outlines for kids to color themselves, then print with AirPrint.

4

Play while Nutmeg steps back

Nutmeg guides the show scene by scene with picture checklists and “pass the iPad to the Head Baker!” hand-offs. Then she takes a nap, and the play happens off the screen.

Saved games and replays

Love a game? Put it on the shelf.

At the end of a game, tap the star to save it. Each saved game gets its own picture on Nutmeg’s bookshelf, ready to play again any time.

  • Play it again with a different crowd. Change the number of players (1 to 6) and the roles and scenes adjust.
  • No waiting. The kit and the play guide are already made, so a replay starts right away. Reprint the kit in color or outline.
  • Nutmeg’s real voice. Replays play back Nutmeg’s own recorded lines from the first time, never a robot voice.
  • Grown-ups stay in charge. Delete saved games in the Grown-ups corner, behind a parental gate.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a printer?

PretendThat is best with one: the custom kit (badges, signs, money and more) is a big part of the fun, and it prints with AirPrint. No printer? Tell Nutmeg, and she’ll guide the kids to draw or make the pieces themselves instead.

What ages is it for?

Kids 4 to 8. It’s voice first with picture buttons, so pre-readers can play, and older kids can take the lead. Up to 6 kids can play one game.

What devices does it run on?

iPad only for now (iPadOS 17 or later). It needs an internet connection to create new games and for Nutmeg’s voice.

What gets saved?

Only games you choose to save, and only the game itself: its title, roles, props, kit pictures, play guide, cover picture and Nutmeg’s recorded lines. Never kids’ names, voices, what they said, or how many played. Saved games stay on your iPad, and you can delete them any time in the Grown-ups corner.

Is it safe for my kids?

Kid-safety rules are built in at every step: Nutmeg steers away from scary, violent or unsafe ideas, never suggests things like the stove, knives or going outside alone, never asks for personal information, and tells kids to get a grown-up if someone seems hurt. What kids type, the game text the AI writes and every picture request are checked before they’re used.
