Talk to Nutmeg

Pick a game by talking

No reading or typing needed.

Kids tell Nutmeg what they want to play, like “a pet hotel”, and she offers ideas, asks how many kids are playing (1 to 6), and gives everyone a role.

  • Voice first with big picture buttons, so kids who can’t read yet can still lead the game
  • Nutmeg never asks for names. She uses roles like “the Captain”
Prop hunt

Find props at home

A blanket becomes the stage curtain.

Nutmeg sends kids on a prop hunt, one thing at a time: a bowl becomes a mixing cauldron, a blanket becomes a stage curtain. Don’t have something? Tap ✗ and she swaps it or you pretend.

  • Pillows, bowls, chairs and blankets. No toys to buy
  • Only soft, safe household things
Printable kit

Print a kit made for your game

Badges, signs, money and more.

A kit drawn just for this game: role badges, a sign, play money, tickets, a menu and cut-outs. Choose full color, or plain outlines for kids to color themselves, then print with AirPrint.

  • Full color, or coloring pages they color in themselves.
  • Nutmeg is an AI helper. The kit is drawn by AI for each new game, and grown-ups decide what gets printed
Play it out

Play it out, scene by scene

Nutmeg sets each scene. Kids act it out.

Nutmeg tells a short story, then guides the setup, three play scenes and the clean-up. Each player gets a row with their picture and their part, which they can tap to hear.

Your bookshelf

Every game saves itself

Printables are always yours to print.

Every game saves itself to Nutmeg’s bookshelf with its own picture. Its printables are always yours to view and print, and with a pass you can play it again with Nutmeg anytime.

  • Printables are always yours. Open any saved game to view and print its kit again.
  • Play it again with a different crowd. With a pass, change the number of players (1 to 6) and the roles and scenes adjust. It never uses up a new game.
  • Grown-ups stay in charge. Delete saved games in the Grown-ups corner, behind a parental gate.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a printer?

PretendThat is best with one: the custom kit (badges, signs, money and more) is a big part of the fun, and it prints with AirPrint.

What ages is it for?

Kids 4 to 8. It’s voice first with picture buttons, so pre-readers can play, and older kids can take the lead. One game is for 1 to 6 kids.

What devices does it run on?

iPad and iPhone (iPadOS or iOS 17 or later). It needs an internet connection to create new games and for Nutmeg’s voice.

What gets saved?

Every new game saves itself once its printable kit is made. Only the game itself is saved: its title, roles, props, kit pictures, play guide, cover picture and Nutmeg’s recorded lines. Never kids’ names, voices, what they said, or how many played. Saved games stay on your device, and you can delete them any time in the Grown-ups corner.

What happens to my saved games if I cancel?

They stay on your bookshelf, and their printables are always yours to view and print. Playing them again with Nutmeg is part of a pass.

Is it safe for my kids?

Kid-safety rules are built in at every step: Nutmeg steers away from scary, violent or unsafe ideas, never suggests things like the stove, knives or going outside alone, never asks for personal information, and tells kids to get a grown-up if someone seems hurt. What kids type, the game text the AI writes and every picture request are checked before they’re used.
