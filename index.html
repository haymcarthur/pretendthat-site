Questions

Good to know

Do I need a printer?

Yes, it’s best with one. The kit prints with AirPrint.

What ages is it for?

Kids 4 to 8, 1 to 6 per game. It’s voice first, so pre-readers can play.

What devices does it run on?

iPad and iPhone (iOS 17 or later), with an internet connection.

What gets saved?

Only the game itself, on your device. Never kids’ names, voices or what they said.

What happens to my saved games if I cancel?

They stay, and their printables are always yours. Replays with Nutmeg need a pass.

Is it safe for my kids?

Yes. Guardrails steer away from anything scary or unsafe, and Nutmeg never asks for personal information.

When can I get it?
