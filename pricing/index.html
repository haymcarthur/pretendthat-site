Questions

Pricing questions

What counts as a “new game”?

A game counts once its printable kit is made. Replaying a saved game never counts.

Does playing a saved game again count?

No. With a pass, replay saved games with Nutmeg as often as you like.

Is the Free Preview every month?

No. It’s 3 free games to try, one time, with no card needed.

Is a yearly plan cheaper?

Yes: 12 months for the price of 10 ($99.99 or $249.99 a year). No intro prices.

What happens if we hit the monthly limit?

Keep replaying saved games. New games open up next month, or switch to the Season Pass.

What happens to my saved games if I cancel?

They stay, and their printables are always yours. Replays with Nutmeg need a pass.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is it a subscription?

Each new game uses AI to write the story and draw the kit, which costs us every time.

Are these prices final?

Not yet. This is our planned pricing, and it may change before launch.
