Pricing questions

What counts as a “new game”?

A new game is one Nutmeg creates from scratch: the kids pick a game, and she writes the story and draws a custom kit for it. Playing a game you saved again is a replay, and replays don’t count toward your monthly new games, even with a different number of players.

What happens if we hit the monthly limit?

You can keep replaying any saved game. New games open up again when your next month starts, or you can switch to a plan with more new games.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are through the App Store, so Apple handles billing. Cancel anytime in your iPad’s Settings under your name › Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is it a subscription?

Each new game uses AI to write the story and draw a custom printable kit, which costs us money every time. Replays reuse what’s already made, so they’re unlimited.

Are these prices final?

Not yet. This is our planned pricing, and it may change before PretendThat launches in the App Store.
