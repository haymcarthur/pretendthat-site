Questions

Pricing questions

What counts as a “new game”?

Each new game is a fresh story and printable kit made just for your kids. Saved games are free to play again. A new game counts once its printable kit is made; closing a game before then doesn’t count. Playing a saved game again doesn’t count toward your monthly new games, even with a different number of players.

Does playing a saved game again count?

No. The monthly limits count new games only. You can play your saved games again as often as you like on every paid plan.

What is an introductory price?

It’s a lower starting price for new subscribers. With the Show Pass you pay $9.99/month for your first 12 months, then $14.99/month. With the Season Pass you pay $19.99/month for your first 12 months, then $29.99/month. After 12 months your subscription renews at the regular price unless you cancel. Yearly plans have an intro price too: the Show Pass is $99.99 for the first year, then $149.99/year, and the Season Pass is $199.99 for the first year, then $299.99/year. You can cancel anytime with your Apple ID (Settings › your name › Subscriptions).

What happens if we hit the monthly limit?

You can keep playing any saved game again. New games open up again when your next month starts, or you can switch from the Show Pass to the Season Pass for more.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are through the App Store, so Apple handles billing. Cancel anytime in your device’s Settings under your name › Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is it a subscription?

Each new game uses AI to write the story and draw a custom printable kit, which costs us money every time. Playing a saved game again reuses what’s already made, so it’s unlimited.

Are these prices final?

Not yet. This is our planned pricing, and it may change before PretendThat launches in the App Store.
