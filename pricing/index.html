Pricing questions

What counts as a “new show”?

A new show is a new game Nutmeg creates from scratch: the kids pick a game, and she writes the story and draws a custom kit for it. Playing a game you saved again is a replay, and replays don’t count toward your monthly new shows, even with a different number of players.

Do the monthly limits count replays?

No. The monthly limits count new shows only. Replays of your saved games are unlimited on every paid plan.

What is an introductory price?

It’s a lower starting price for new subscribers. With the Show Pass you pay $9.99/month for your first 12 months, then $14.99/month. With the Season Pass you pay $19.99/month for your first 12 months, then $24.99/month. After 12 months your subscription renews at the regular price unless you cancel. You can cancel anytime with your Apple ID (Settings › your name › Subscriptions). Yearly plans are billed at the regular yearly price.

What happens if we hit the monthly limit?

You can keep replaying any saved game. New shows open up again when your next month starts, or you can switch from the Show Pass to the Season Pass for more.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are through the App Store, so Apple handles billing. Cancel anytime in your iPad’s Settings under your name › Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is it a subscription?

Each new show uses AI to write the story and draw a custom printable kit, which costs us money every time. Replays reuse what’s already made, so they’re unlimited.

Are these prices final?

Not yet. This is our planned pricing, and it may change before PretendThat launches in the App Store.
