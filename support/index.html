Quick answers

Good to know

Nutmeg can’t hear us

Hold the red microphone button while you talk, then let go. Make sure PretendThat is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition (Settings › PretendThat) and that the device is online. If the microphone looks gray with a line through it, the device can’t understand speech on the device yet; you can type to Nutmeg instead. Still stuck? Close PretendThat and open it again.

The kit won’t print

PretendThat prints with AirPrint. Check that the printer is on, on the same Wi-Fi as the iPad or iPhone, and shows up in the print screen.

Color or outline pages?

Every kit is drawn both ways. On the Print screen, tap the set you want (full color, or outlines to color in), or just tell Nutmeg, then tap the printer.

How do I save, play again or delete a game?

Every new game saves itself to the bookshelf once its printable kit is made; there’s no save button. Tap Saved games on the start screen and pick a game: its printables are always yours to view and print, and with a pass you can play it again with Nutmeg. To delete a saved game, open the Grown-ups corner on the start screen (hold the button, then answer the number question) and use Saved games.

Nutmeg says she needs the internet

New games and new lines need a connection. Connect to Wi-Fi and the game picks up where it paused.

Billing, refunds and subscriptions

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund or report a problem with a purchase, use reportaproblem.apple.com. You can manage subscriptions in your device’s Settings under your name › Subscriptions.
