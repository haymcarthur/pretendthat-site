Support

How can we help?

Check the quick answers below, or send us a message and we’ll get back to you by email.

Quick answers

Nutmeg can’t hear us

Hold the red microphone button while you talk, then let go. Make sure PretendThat is allowed to use the microphone (iPad Settings › PretendThat › Microphone) and that the iPad is online. Still stuck? Close PretendThat and open it again.

The kit won’t print

PretendThat prints with AirPrint. Check that the printer is on, on the same Wi-Fi as the iPad, and shows up in the print screen. No printer? Tell Nutmeg, and she’ll help the kids draw or make the pieces instead.

Color or outline pages?

Every kit is drawn both ways. On the Print screen, tap the set you want (full color, or outlines to color in), or just tell Nutmeg, then tap the printer.

How do I save, replay or delete a game?

At the end of a game, tap the star to save it to the bookshelf. Tap Saved games on the start screen to play it again. To delete a saved game, open the Grown-ups corner on the start screen (hold the button, then answer the number question) and use Saved games.

Nutmeg says she needs the internet

New games and new lines need a connection. Connect the iPad to Wi-Fi and the game picks up where it paused.

Billing, refunds and subscriptions

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund or report a problem with a purchase, use reportaproblem.apple.com. You can manage subscriptions in your iPad’s Settings under your name › Subscriptions.

Contact us

Send a question, report a bug, or say hi. We reply to the email address you give us. Please don’t include personal details about your kids.

Billing and refunds for App Store purchases go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Messages are delivered to our support inbox by the FormSubmit service. See our privacy policy. You can also email support@pretendthat.app.
