Quick answers

Good to know

Nutmeg can’t hear us

Hold the red mic button while you talk, and allow the microphone and speech recognition in Settings › PretendThat. A gray, crossed-out mic means speech isn’t ready on the device yet, so type to Nutmeg instead.

The kit won’t print

Check that the printer is on, on the same Wi-Fi, and shows up for AirPrint.

Color or outline pages?

Every kit comes both ways. Pick a set on the Print screen, or just tell Nutmeg.

How do I save, play again or delete a game?

Games save themselves. Open Saved games to print or replay (with a pass), and delete them in the Grown-ups corner.

Nutmeg says she needs the internet

New games need a connection. Reconnect and the game picks up where it paused.

Billing, refunds and subscriptions

Apple handles App Store billing: use reportaproblem.apple.com. Manage subscriptions in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
